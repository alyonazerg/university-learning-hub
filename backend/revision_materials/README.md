Unit 7 draft materials prepared from the five teacher-provided vocabulary PDFs and grammar coverage. No student names are included here. The migration creates closed drafts; publication is a teacher action.

Each group receives 12 vocabulary, 6 collocation, 10 grammar and 6 group-specific emergent items plus an essay of about 250 words. Group 216 receives an additional article item instead of another/other. Scores normalise section accuracy to 40/25/15/20 points. Question wording and accepted alternatives should be reviewed by the teacher before use.

Experimental allocation: 213 and 215; control: 214, 216, 217. Allocation stays attached to attempts even if group settings change. Research consent is independent of allocation: declining participation does not move a student into control. Declining suppresses the reading preview and excludes the submission from research exports. Academic exports remain available. Withdrawal using the attempt's secret token removes the submission from future research exports; the teacher must also remove it from any already downloaded research copies.

Class lists and individual nonattendance flags are configured privately in the teacher cabinet. Expected attendance is not evidence of an actual submission. Absent students are not assigned zero scores.

Reaction time is measured from item display to first input, retained on edits and persisted across reloads. It measures browser interaction, not a controlled laboratory response: interrupted sessions, device differences and client-supplied timings require screening. MCQ times and grammar times are stored but excluded from lexical gap RT aggregates. Free-response target use uses exact phrases and explicit variants, excludes copied question/preview sentences, and requires teacher review of usage quality.

AI feedback is optional: configure REVISION_AI_API_KEY, REVISION_AI_MODEL and REVISION_AI_BASE_URL in the private server environment. The provider receives the writing prompt, level and response, without the student's name. Provisional AI marks never become final automatically; teacher writing review is required. Without a configured provider, writing remains awaiting manual review.

A revision attempt is accessible using an opaque per-attempt bearer token, stored in the browser, not a platform account. Losing browser storage loses student access; the teacher retains the submission. Do not share tokens or place class lists in this repository.


Speaking uses twelve individual prompts and six dialogue situations per level, adapted from the teacher's actual Unit 7 slides, including beyond-coursebook debates. Questions and sources are documented in unit7-*-speaking.md. Individual answers begin when the teacher calls the student; the student reveals the question then. The teacher saves all four individual criteria for each participant, then assigns the pair explicitly through Pair students. Each phone receives only its own role. Both dialogue grades are entered side by side and saved together. Reading lasts 30 seconds, preparation one minute, and the dialogue 2–3 minutes. An odd participant can be paired with the teacher. Oral scores are stored separately out of 20; the existing written score remains out of 100. No audio recording is performed. Research exports apply consent exclusions to oral scores too.
