Teacher Evolve 5 Unit 7 PRIORITIES slides: SB pp.65,67,69,71,73–74; Meaningful Work debate; negotiation debate; money and happiness; consumer behaviour. Questions are adapted assessment prompts, not verbatim coursebook tasks.

Essay (about 250 words)
Write an essay of about 250 words: Meaningful work matters more than a high salary. To what extent do you agree? Consider both perspectives, support your argument with an example, and give a reasoned conclusion. Use your own words. Do not copy the reading preview or task sentences.

Individual answer: no preparation, 60–90 seconds
1. What makes an experience worthwhile? Give an example and explain your priorities.
2. What makes a job fulfilling beyond its salary?
3. Why can having too many choices make decisions harder?
4. Can spending money on other people make us happier than spending it on ourselves? Explain.
5. How do shops encourage people to buy things they did not plan to buy?
6. When is cooperation more effective than competition in a negotiation?

Dialogue: one minute preparation, 2–3 minutes discussion
1. Two graduates are choosing between a secure, well-paid job and meaningful work with lower pay. Compare the priorities, discuss risks, and agree on advice. Student A prioritises financial security; Student B prioritises fulfilment.
2. Plan a worthwhile day out for your class with a limited budget. Student A prefers an experience; Student B prefers something practical that lasts. Agree on one plan and justify the compromise.
3. An employee wants a pay rise, but the manager has a limited budget. Negotiate a fair solution including at least one non-financial benefit. Student A is the employee; Student B is the manager.
4. Your university wants to reduce impulsive shopping among students. One of you proposes restrictions on advertising; the other proposes consumer education. Discuss benefits and drawbacks and agree on two measures.
