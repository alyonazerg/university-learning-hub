## Shared rules confirmed by the teacher

- IMT and extra tasks are OPTIONAL in both courses. Not selected/not submitted is not an overdue required assignment, a debt or an automatically awarded zero. Earned academic credit still contributes within the relevant category caps; optional does not mean that its maximum should silently be redistributed to other categories.
- Distinguish a published option, a student selecting/reserving it, a draft, submission, review, requested revision and final teacher-approved grade. Withdrawal and an agreed presentation date need explicit handling.
- Keep ordinary homework's required/optional flag independent. No assumption that all homework is optional.
- AI analyses evidence and proposes criterion marks and feedback. Teacher confirms final credit. Oral checks, live facilitation and contribution cannot be inferred from an uploaded file alone.
- Group submissions keep one shared evidence record, participant accounts and contribution statements, with an individually confirmed credit award to each participant. No automatic same-mark assumption; no duplicate awards for repeated uploads or approval retries.
- Audio/video evidence may be uploaded privately. External AI audio processing requires the existing explicit consent/configuration boundary; this catalogue does not authorize enabling it or sending recordings externally.

## Automatic updates and traceability

1. Teacher approves an assignment assessment → one versioned official award updates its activity cell, category subtotal and semester total.
2. Recorded attendance → updates only the private teacher attendance view. It produces no award. A separately entered assessment of participation/answers can update its configured academic category; it is never inferred from presence.
3. Assessment change, revision, move, pause or retry → preserve evidence and history; recalculate using policy rules without duplicate credit.
4. AI pending/failed, not selected optional, submitted awaiting review and an explicit teacher mark of 0 remain distinct states.
5. Clicking a cell shows source work, criterion breakdown, comments, approval time and correction history. Student sees only their own academic breakdown; public voluntary XP ranking stays separate.
